Making a game — the short version

Just 4 easy steps

What this does

You give it a topic — paste in some notes, or just type what you want (like "Irish verbs" or "our new starter checklist") — and it turns that into a game with questions to practise.

How you move through it

  • 1Pick a category and give your game a name
  • 2Tell it what the game should be about
  • 3Play it yourself first, to make sure it's right
  • 4Add it to the library so others can play too

Tip

Big gold buttons move you to the next step. You can also tap the words along the top (Basics, Content, Preview, Add to Library) to jump around.

What's in it?

Two ways in: paste real content (an article, a policy doc, a vocab list) and we'll build questions from it — or write a short prompt describing a topic and we'll generate it from scratch. You can also do both: paste content and add a line asking us to expand or vary it.
